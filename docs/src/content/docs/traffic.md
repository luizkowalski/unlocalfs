---
title: Traffic
description: See how much data each drive uploaded and downloaded.
---

Select **Traffic** at the top of the sidebar to see how much data your drives moved over the network. Choose **Today**, **This Week**, **This Month**, **This Year**, or **Custom** and pick a start and end date.

The page shows the total for all drives, split into upload and download. The table lists each drive with its total, a bar for its share, and its upload and download. Drives that moved nothing in the period show 0 bytes. A deleted drive still shows in periods where it moved data.

- Weeks start on the first day of the week set in **System Settings → General → Language & Region**. Days follow your Mac's time zone.
- Only data that moves over the network counts. Opening a file that is already in the cache, renaming or moving files in Finder, and **Duplicate** do not count.
- Previews count as downloads, because Finder reads the file to show them.
- UnlocalFS saves the numbers every hour, when a drive disconnects, and when a new day starts. If the app quits unexpectedly while drives stay connected, it catches up the next time it opens. If your Mac shuts down unexpectedly, up to an hour of traffic can be lost.
- Traffic history starts when you install this version, so the first month and year show less than your real use.
- The numbers count file data only. Your provider's bill can be higher, because it also counts requests and folder listings.
